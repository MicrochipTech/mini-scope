# Card 1.2: virtual motor on the port SIM

Base: commit "1.1 accepted". Changed: `tests/mock/sim.c`, `tests/sim_serial.py`, new `docs/evidence/m1/sim_start.py`.
`scope.c`, `tune_model.h` and `example_main.c` are not changed.

## What changed

- `sim.c` includes `tune_model.h`. `motor`, `startup` and `tune_alive` are macros onto `mock_ram`
  (0x1100 motor, 0x1120 startup, 0x1128 tune_alive), with the same member order as `example_main.c`.
  `sim_init` sets the same defaults as `example_main.c` and clears the model state.
- `sim_run(ticks)` is replaced by `sim_run_us(us)`. The DLL keeps the virtual time. It handles two kinds of
  events in time order: `tune_model_step()` every 100 us, and the Timer1 interrupt at the period the
  firmware set (`sim_period_us()`, any value, a timer stopped by the firmware stops the events).
  The model therefore runs while Timer1 is stopped. On equal time the Timer1 event comes first.
- `sim_serial.py`: `_advance` passes the elapsed wall time in us (capped at 2 s as before in effect) to the
  DLL. Time still runs with the wall clock, factor 1, as before. `SYMBOLS` has all 13 `motor.*` members,
  3 `startup.*` members and `tune_alive`, each `(address, 2)`, all 16 bit. Signedness is chosen in the GUI
  per channel, as for the demo symbols.

How each point is compared:

- Start: state change times are taken from the 1 ms stream (sample index) and compared with the rows of
  `model_check.txt` (rate 100: 1/101/161 ms; rate 400: 1/101, fault 2 at 102 ms), all relative to the first
  state change 0 to 1, because the absolute time of the `run` write is only known to a few samples (the line
  "run written at about sample" shows it). Differences are 0 ms for ramp, closed loop and fault; limit was 2 ms.
- Model without sample timer: `sim_period_us()` is 0 during the 0.5 s after writing `current_ref = 3000`. The
  first sample of the stream started afterwards already reads 3000.
- Capture, step 500 at `current_kp` 400, `current_ki` 20, trigger on `current_ref`: rise t10 to t90 is 500 us
  at 10 us and at 100 us period, `model_check.txt` says 500 us. At 10 us the value changes every 10 samples
  (= one 100 us tick), at 100 us every sample. Trigger index 102 in both (10 % pretrigger).
  The 10 us capture spans 10.24 ms and is not settled at its end (last sample 483), so t90 is taken against the
  step of 500, not against the last sample.
- GUI names: `gui/scope_gui.py` builds its channel and write dropdowns from `sim_symbols()` with
  `{n: f'{n}  (0x{a:04X}, {s} B)' for n, (a, s) in syms.items()}`. The script calls `g.sim_symbols()` and builds
  the same expression, and checks that 23 names are there (all new ones plus the demo symbols). This is
  not the browser: the NiceGUI page itself was not opened.
